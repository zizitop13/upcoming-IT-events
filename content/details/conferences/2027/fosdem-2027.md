# FOSDEM 2027

**30–31 January 2027 · Brussels · On-site · Free**

FOSDEM is one of Europe's largest developer-oriented free and open-source conferences. The conference language is English and recorded material is published under an open licence.

## Useful CFP dates

- Main tracks: **16 November 2026**
- Free Java devroom: **29 November 2026**
- Testing and Continuous Delivery devroom: **1 December 2026**

The Free Java room is particularly relevant for JVM/OpenJDK topics. Testing and Continuous Delivery accepts 25-minute talks and 10-minute lightning talks.

## Constraint

Talk content must be connected to free/open-source software. A purely internal proprietary case study is not a good fit unless it can be grounded in reusable open-source work.
