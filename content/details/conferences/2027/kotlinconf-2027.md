# KotlinConf 2027

**21–23 April 2027 · Kraków · On-site**

KotlinConf is the official Kotlin community conference organised by JetBrains. Workshops take place on 21 April, followed by the main conference on 22–23 April.

## CFP

- CFP closes: **27 November 2026, 23:59 CET**
- Audience: 2,000+ Kotlin engineers
- Good topics include production Kotlin, coroutines, frameworks, architecture, performance and lessons learned from real systems.

## Why it is useful

This is one of the highest-signal speaking and networking opportunities in the list for engineers interested in JetBrains-level product companies. It is still an indirect hiring signal: no interview fast-track is promised.
