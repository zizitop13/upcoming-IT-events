# ITCS Frankfurt 2026

**27 November 2026 · Messe Frankfurt · On-site · Free**

ITCS combines a tech conference, career fair and festival in one day. The published programme includes more than 40 talks, and the exhibition floor is designed for direct conversations with technical teams and employers.

## Why it is useful

- Local to Frankfurt, so travel cost is effectively zero.
- Explicit career-fair component for both professionals and students.
- Company booths are staffed for discussions about projects and career opportunities.
- Afterparty begins after the conference programme.

## Schedule

- Doors: 09:30
- Conference and career fair: 10:00–16:00
- Afterparty: from 16:00
