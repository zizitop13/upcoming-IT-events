# Junction 2026

**13–15 November 2026 · Espoo, Finland · On-site · Free**

Junction is a 48-hour hackathon for around 2,000 builders from more than 100 countries. The published prize pool is **€100,000**.

## Application status

Applications closed on **25 October 2026**. This row remains visible because the event itself is still upcoming, which is useful for people who already applied.

## Why it is useful

Strong company challenges, public demos and a large international builder community make Junction a high-signal portfolio/networking event. Recruiting exposure is real but not a guaranteed interview path.
