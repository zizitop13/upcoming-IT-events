# 12th CASSINI Hackathon — Germany

**27–29 November 2026 · Darmstadt · On-site · Free**

The Darmstadt edition of the EU-funded CASSINI Hackathon focuses on peace, resilience and security using European space assets such as Copernicus, Galileo and EGNOS.

## Deadlines and format

- Registration deadline: **20 November 2026**
- Check-in on Friday: from 17:00
- Kick-off: 18:00
- Final submission: **29 November at 15:00**
- Teams can be formed from individual participants; prior space experience is not required.

## Challenges

- Cybersecurity for space-based strategic enablers
- Space-enabled crisis response and civil resilience
- Countering transnational crime and terrorism with space data

## Prizes

Local prizes are €1,500 / €1,000 / €500. Local winners can qualify for the European competition, where the central prizes are €5,000 / €3,000 / €1,000, plus mentoring opportunities.
