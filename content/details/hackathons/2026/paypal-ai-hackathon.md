# PayPal AI Hackathon

**1 October–12 November 2026 · Online · Free**

PayPal's global AI hackathon asks participants to build a working product that meaningfully combines **PayPal technology** with **AI**. There are no fixed problem tracks: an entry can be an agent, payment experience, automation, business tool, app, or another original product.

## Deadline

Final submissions close on **12 November 2026 at 12:00 PST**, which is **21:00 CET** in Germany.

Winners are scheduled to be announced around **21 December 2026**.

## Team rules

- You can participate **solo or as a team**.
- The official rules do **not set a maximum team size**.
- Existing projects are allowed if meaningful progress is made during the hackathon period.
- Participants must meet the age-of-majority and country eligibility requirements in the official rules.

## Project requirements

The submission must:

- meaningfully integrate at least one PayPal API, SDK, product, technology, or developer capability;
- meaningfully use AI;
- provide a working prototype or proof of concept;
- include enough documentation for judges to understand and run it;
- use a **public open-source repository** with a visible license;
- include either a hosted working demo or complete setup/run instructions;
- include a **public demo video under 3 minutes** showing the product working.

A static mock-up is not sufficient.

## Prizes

Devpost currently lists **$67,500 in cash prizes**, including:

- 1st place — **$12,000**
- 2nd place — **$8,000**
- 3rd place — **$5,000**
- Most Creative — **$5,000**
- Most Impactful — **$5,000**
- Best Demo Delivery — **$5,000**
- Best Use of PayPal + AI — **$5,000**
- Best Use of Agentic Commerce — **$5,000**

There are also sponsor awards from AG Grid, APIMatic, Bryntum, Channel3 and Render. Some sponsor awards are credits rather than cash, which is why promotional totals can differ from the cash total.

## Useful technologies and partners

The hackathon provides or highlights tools from PayPal, AG Grid, APIMatic, Astropods, Bryntum, Channel3, Elastic, Kernel, Postman, Render and Zapier.

For a backend/fintech project, the most interesting engineering areas are agentic payments, idempotency, transaction consistency, approval/risk limits, retries, auditability and safe execution of AI-initiated financial actions.

## Recruiting value

The project is judged in the PayPal ecosystem and gives a public GitHub artifact plus a demo that can be reused in a portfolio. This is **indirect recruiting value**; the organizers do not promise an interview or hiring fast-track.
