# Codegeist 2026

**27 October–22 December 2026 · Online · Free**

Atlassian's Codegeist is an online Forge hackathon for applications extending products such as Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket and Rovo.

## Submission

- Registration and submission window closes: **22 December 2026**
- A working project submission is required.
- Total published cash prize pool: **$120,000**.

Because it is fully online and judged by the Atlassian ecosystem, a strong public submission can be a useful portfolio signal. It does not promise a hiring interview.
